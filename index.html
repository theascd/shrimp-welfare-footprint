Shrimp Welfare Footprint

Choose a farming method, a country and any welfare interventions. The page estimates cumulative pain per shrimp by intensity, compares it with the same system under current practice, and shows the range, the sources and the confidence behind each number.

Who is this page for
  • Animal welfare researchers and advocates comparing farming systems, countries and interventions on one scale.
  • Funders and analysts deciding which interventions or countries deserve attention first.
  • Producers, buyers and certifiers who want a rough sense of how farming choices change the welfare burden.

Not for farm audits, compliance or a final decision on its own: the figures are estimates with wide ranges, built on Rethink Priorities’ expert intervals.

How to use this page
  1. Pick a farming method in step 1 (left): extensive, semi-intensive, intensive, super-intensive or indoor RAS, or “Typical mix for this country” to weight the systems by the country’s production.
  2. Choose a country in step 2. Ten are modelled with their own conditions. For any other country pick “Other country…” and type its name: it is matched to the modelled country with the most similar farm types. Optionally enter your production in tonnes.
  3. Tick welfare interventions in step 3, or none to see today’s baseline. Ticked ones are treated as fully adopted; where the country already uses one, only the remaining gap counts.
  4. Read the results on the right. They update at once: pain hours per shrimp with a 90% range, the change from baseline, the split by intensity, and totals scaled to national output.
  5. Change what counts as pain with the “Count pain at” buttons above the results (any intensity up to excruciating, or disabling-equivalent hours), and the scale and sentience adjustments under Assumptions.
  6. Check the evidence. Each number carries a confidence label; click a bracket such as [101] for the full citation, a link and a copy button. The sensitivity tables and the strongest objections are further down.

Every figure is an estimate with a range, not a measurement. Use Show all / Hide all below to open or close sections.

Page sections
What the numbers rest on

Baseline pain. Prevalence, daily durations and intensity splits per farming system come from Rethink Priorities (2024), the only published quantitative source for farmed shrimp. They are expert credible intervals, not measurements. The page checks itself against that report’s published total.

Country conditions. Production, species mix, harvest size, cycle length, stocking density, aeration and disease prevalence come from national statistics, peer-reviewed surveys, Seafood Watch assessments and trade data. Where sources disagree, the page uses their average and shows the spread and every source value.

Intervention effects. No source quantifies how much an intervention reduces each threat in affective terms. Those effect sizes are analyst estimates, marked est, and the supporting experiments are listed beside each one.
