Shrimp Welfare Footprint

Choose a farming method, a country and any welfare interventions. The page estimates cumulative pain per shrimp by intensity, compares it with the same system under current practice, and shows the range, the sources and the confidence behind each number.

Page sections
What the numbers rest on

Baseline pain. Prevalence, daily durations and intensity splits per farming system come from Rethink Priorities (2024), the only published quantitative source for farmed shrimp. They are expert credible intervals, not measurements. The page checks itself against that report’s published total.

Country conditions. Production, species mix, harvest size, cycle length, stocking density, aeration and disease prevalence come from national statistics, peer-reviewed surveys, Seafood Watch assessments and trade data. Where sources disagree, the page uses their average and shows the spread and every source value.

Intervention effects. No source quantifies how much an intervention reduces each threat in affective terms. Those effect sizes are analyst estimates, marked est, and the supporting experiments are listed beside each one.
